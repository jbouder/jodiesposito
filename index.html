About Jodi

Jodi Esposito is Director of Digital Marketing at Reading Is Fundamental, where she leads digital strategy, content, search, and analytics across multiple web properties and the Skybrary reading app.

Her career spans more than 15 years with national associations and advocacy organizations, including the National Music Publishers' Association, the U.S. Chamber Institute for Legal Reform, and the Copyright Alliance, where she built marketing programs that grew audiences, membership, and search visibility. She pairs strategy with hands-on execution, from SEO and AI search to events, design, and earned media.
